Paleoart Workshop by Joschua Knüppe

Event Overview

On 27 March 2026, we welcomed paleoartist Joschua Knüppe to Utrecht University for an afternoon of exploring the art and science of paleoart.

The workshop focused on how to create convincing paleoart — and how to recognise it. We discussed the importance of using reliable sources, from fossils and scientific publications to information about ancient environments. Joschua also showed us how artistic choices can bring a reconstruction to life, guide the viewer’s attention, and turn scientific information into an engaging image.

One of the things we explored was how the purpose of a reconstruction influences the choices you make. A scientific illustration, a museum display, and a more informal artwork may all require different approaches to accuracy and artistic freedom. It was a great reminder that good paleoart is not just about drawing extinct animals, but about understanding what you are trying to communicate.

After the presentation, we got to put some of these ideas into practice ourselves. Joschua had brought plenty of drawing materials, sketches, fossil models, and books to explore. There was lots of room to experiment, exchange ideas, and ask questions about techniques and ongoing projects. It was a busy and inspiring afternoon, with a great turnout and plenty of enthusiasm for getting creative!
