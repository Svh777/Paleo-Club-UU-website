About Us

Welcome to the Paleo Club UU. We are a community for students at Utrecht University with a shared passion for paleontology. Founded in 2024, we bring together people fascinated by life’s history on Earth, from microscopic fossils to the largest dinosaurs and whales.

With an enthusiastic board and a growing group of over 80 active members, we regularly organize lectures, symposia, and workshops, alongside informal events where students can connect and exchange ideas. Whether you’re just starting out or already deeply engaged in paleontology, our club offers a place to explore, learn, and meet like-minded people. > read more
