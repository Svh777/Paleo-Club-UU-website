A Peek Inside the Fossil Botany Collection

Event Overview

On 28 January 2026, we got the chance to take a closer look at Utrecht University’s fossil botany collection at the Tolakker. Guided by Friederike Wagner-Cremer, we explored plant fossils from the Carboniferous coal forests, as well as Devonian material, and learned more about the plants that once shaped these ancient environments.

We saw ferns, lycopsids such as Lepidodendron, horsetails, and many more fossil plants. Along the way, Friederike shared fascinating insights into paleobotany and helped us identify and sort the specimens. We also got to try our hand at bringing some order to the collection, grouping fossil boxes by outcrop and searching for specimens that belonged together.

By the end of the afternoon, we had helped sort more than 15 pallets of fossil boxes! And, as an added bonus, we were even allowed to take some fossils home for our own collections. It was a great opportunity to explore a part of the university’s collections that many of us had never seen before — and to get our hands dirty doing it.
