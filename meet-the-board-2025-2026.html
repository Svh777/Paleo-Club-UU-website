Meet the Board

The board is made up of students from Utrecht University who help keep the club running throughout the year. From organizing lectures, workshops, symposia and social events to managing the website, communication and everything in between, there’s quite a bit that happens behind the scenes.

We all have our own interests within paleontology, but share the same enthusiasm for bringing students together around the subject. Get to know the board members below and find out who is behind the Paleo Club UU.
