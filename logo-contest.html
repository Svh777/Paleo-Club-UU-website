Logo Contest

Event Overview

At the beginning of 2026, we invited our members to take part in a paleo-art contest to design a new logo for the Paleo Club UU. The contest brought together a wide range of creative ideas and showcased the artistic talent within our community.

We received many impressive submissions, each offering a different interpretation of what the Paleo Club could look like. After the submission period, the entries were put to a vote, and Marielle Greup’s design was chosen as the winning logo.

Her design combines a classic ammonite motif with the name of the club, creating a logo that connects the club to paleontology while remaining simple and recognisable. We were delighted with the result and have adopted it as the official Paleo Club UU logo.
