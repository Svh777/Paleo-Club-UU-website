Whale Symposium

Event Overview

On 24 February 2025, we organised a symposium exploring whales from the living animals we know today to their extinct relatives and evolutionary history.

The afternoon began with talks focusing on modern whales and strandings, providing an introduction to the biology and natural history of living cetaceans. From there, we worked our way back through time, exploring historical whales and the fossil record.

The symposium finished with extinct whales and their evolution, bringing together the different perspectives from the day to show how the history of whales can be reconstructed through both living animals and fossils.
