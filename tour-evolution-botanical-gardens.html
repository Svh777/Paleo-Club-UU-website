Tour Evolution – Botanical Gardens

Event Overview

In spring 2025, we were given an exclusive guided tour of the Evolution Garden at the Utrecht University Botanic Gardens, shortly before it opened to the public.

Dr. Edwin Post, Scientific Director of the gardens, took us on a journey through plant evolution, following the major changes that allowed plants to establish themselves on land and diversify into the forms we see today. We began with early groups such as algae and mosses and then explored subsequent adaptations, including increasing vertical growth, drought resistance, and the coevolution of plants with other organisms.

Throughout the tour, Dr. Post encouraged us to ask questions and discuss different aspects of plant evolution. Rather than simply presenting the garden as a collection of plants, he challenged us to look at the evolutionary processes behind them and to think critically about how these adaptations shaped the history of life on land.

Approaching the subject from an evolutionary biology perspective gave us a fascinating way to explore plant evolution, and the tour provided plenty of opportunities to connect what we saw in the garden with broader questions about the history of life.
