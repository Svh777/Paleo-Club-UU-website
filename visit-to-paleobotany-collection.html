Visit to the Paleobotany Collection

Event Overview

On 19 November 2024, we held a meeting focused on fossil plants and the history of plant evolution.

The evening began with a talk by Friederieke Wagner-Cremer, who introduced us to plant evolution and the fossil record of plants. We then continued with a guided visit to the Utrecht University collections, led by Friederieke Wagner-Cremer and Iris Kuipers.

The collection visit gave us the opportunity to explore the specimens up close and learn more about the material held at Utrecht University. After the guided tour, we also had time to look around the collections ourselves and discover the specimens that caught our attention.
